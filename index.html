Our Journey

Born From a Soaked City Street

It all started on a soaked city street when yet another cheap store-bought umbrella flipped inside out during a heavy gust of wind, leaving us drenched and frustrated. We realized rainwear hadn't evolved in decades—floppy frames, single-use plastics, and disappointing design were accepted as the norm.

Driven by a passion for architectural precision and modern materials, we set out to re-engineer rainwear from the ground up. By blending carbon-fiber anti-inversion ribs, nano-hydrophobic stealth fabrics, and ergonomic luxury handles, we created umbrellas engineered to perform under extreme conditions.
